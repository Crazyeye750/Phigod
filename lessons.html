NJ Sculpting Lessons

Learn stone carving in Manalapan, NJ

Stone carving is one of the oldest arts in the world — and one of the most rewarding to learn. At her Manalapan studio, Jacqui Melman teaches aspiring sculptors of every level, drawing on nearly a decade of training under renowned artist Elaine Warshaw.

Classes are small and hands-on. No experience is necessary: if you can hold a chisel, you can learn to carve.

Ask About Enrollment
What You'll Learn

From raw stone to finished form

  • Reading the stone — choosing a block, understanding grain, and seeing the form inside it.
  • Tools and technique — safe, confident handling of points, chisels, rasps, and rifflers.
  • Roughing out — removing material with purpose, and learning when to stop.
  • Refining and finishing — surfaces, textures, sanding, and polish.
  • Developing your eye — composition, negative space, and the modernist tradition from de Creeft onward.
"Every student's first finished piece is my favorite thing in the studio that week."
