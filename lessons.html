NJ Sculpting Lessons

Learn stone carving in Manalapan, NJ

Stone carving is one of the oldest arts in the world — and one of the most rewarding to learn.

At her Manalapan studio, Jacqui teaches aspiring sculptors of every level, drawing on nearly a decade of training under renowned artist Elaine Warshaw. Classes are small and hands-on. No experience is necessary: if you can hold a chisel, you can learn to carve.

Ask About Enrollment

Curriculum

From raw stone to finished form

  1. Reading the stone

    Choosing a block, understanding grain, and seeing the form inside it.

  2. Tools and technique

    Safe, confident handling of points, chisels, rasps, and rifflers.

  3. Roughing out

    Removing material with purpose — and learning when to stop.

  4. Refining and finishing

    Surfaces, textures, sanding, and polish.

  5. Developing your eye

    Composition, negative space, and the modernist tradition from de Creeft onward.
