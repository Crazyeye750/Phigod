Stone, color, and a divine sense of humor.

Jacqui Melman carves, paints, and draws with an intensity tempered by whimsy — and teaches the ancient craft of stone carving from her New Jersey studio.

Three ways into one vision

I.

Sculpture

Inspired by modernism and the stonework of José de Creeft — the most classical thread of the practice. Patient, direct, and alive.

II.

Painting

Bold, oversized canvases shaped by cubists like Max Weber and Georges Braque, with the color sense of a lifelong student of the palette.

III.

Pastels & Drawings

Expressive works on paper where intensity meets whimsy — the freest, most immediate line of a restless imagination.

Learn Stone Carving

A school for aspiring sculptors in Manalapan, NJ

After nearly a decade under renowned artist Elaine Warshaw, Jacqui opened her own school. Students learn to read the stone, handle the tools, and discover the form waiting inside.
